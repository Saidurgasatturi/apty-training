Delicious Chocolate Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 and 1/2 cups sugar
  • 3/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 and 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 and 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup boiling water

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two cake pans.
  2. In a bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking soda, and baking powder.
  3. Add eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla. Mix until smooth.
  4. Slowly stir in boiling water until batter is well mixed.
  5. Pour batter into pans and bake for 30–35 minutes.
  6. Let cakes cool before frosting and serving.

A rich and moist chocolate cake, perfect for celebrations.
